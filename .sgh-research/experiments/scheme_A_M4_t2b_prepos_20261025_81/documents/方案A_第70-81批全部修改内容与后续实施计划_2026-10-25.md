#### `t2_scenario_driver_v1.py`(各版本树根目录)

**作用**:T2场景族编排Driver;普通scope(v3合同)与boundary scope(v4合同)共用。

**核心流程**:
1. `registration_preflight_t2()`入口预检(合同SHA/bundle pin/ELF pin/registry/表SHA/输出目录)
2. 创建Driver manifest/pins(create-only)
3. Spawn四角色(fixture/worker/recorder/regulator),逐一`authenticate()`
4. Boundary模式:在Worker spawn前打开私有pipe,read端经`pass_fds`继承,write端由Driver持有
5. 写`fixture_start.json`(v2 schema:T2字段绑定)
6. Ready→grant→prepared链
7. 观察循环:
   - Regulator Source(先读):`job`(jobs_by_id)/`path_version`(boundary释放条件)/`candidate_registration`(ready_jobs)
   - Worker Source(后读):`job_received`(嵌套job.job_id→received_jobs)/`w2_boundary_entered`(直接填active_jobs)/`capability_publish_attempt`(active_job)/`candidate`(generated_jobs)
   - Fixture Source:`t2_action_executed`回执
   - Boundary释放逻辑:Fixture回执+Regulator path_version ACK齐→写44字节frame(magic/version/job/ordinal/nonce/single-use/CRC32)
   - 触发评估:完整证据→命令(create-only文件)→排水(精确集合)
8. Close ladder:regulator→fixture→worker→recorder(SIGINT阶梯);T2-C停止回执绑定实际SIGINT sent_ns

**关键设计决策**:
- **job_received预置触发**(rev12):path动作在boundary进入前~1ms预置;boundary_entered作为在途证据而非触发——解决外部Driver文件编排200ms>150ms deadline的架构约束
- **C停止请求回执绑定实际SIGINT时刻**(R0):不预发标签;在close ladder中发给regulator的第一个SIGINT的sent_ns即为request_ns
- **动态scope/contract_hash**(R2-Driver):全部`ORDINARY_SCOPE`/`V3_CONTRACT_SHA`硬编码→`reg['scope']`/`reg['contract_sha256']`
- **boundary pipe**(R2):spawn前`os.pipe()`→Worker继承read→Driver持write→Fixture回执+path_version齐后释放→finally关闭

## 三、架构决策记录

### 3.1 T2能力=普通scope+非fault(v1-v8)→boundary scope+v4(rev9+)

T2最初设计为普通scope(v3合同)+非fault四角色。R2引入boundary scope(v4合同+fault injection)以复用W2边界机制。两种scope共用同一Driver,通过registration的`scope`字段区分。

### 3.2 外部Driver时序约束(第80批)

**实测**:Python观察循环~140ms(3 SourceCursor文件读+4进程poll)+ 命令写入~52ms(fsync) + 动作执行~7ms = **~199ms > 150ms Job deadline**。

**结论**:外部Driver基于文件I/O的编排架构上不可能在150ms内完成及时在途场景。

**解决**(第81批):job_received预置触发——path动作在boundary进入前~1ms预置;boundary_entered作为在途证据;释放后C调用产生的候选被version_mismatch拒绝。语义完整,时序无冲突。

### 3.3 Regulator源码SHA不变量让位(第76批)

66批以来Regulator源码SHA(`dd03d769…`)的不变量在第76批让位于已证明的实现缺陷(R1修复)。新SHA:`244c08a1…`。74批§2明确"不能因源码SHA没变而忽略"。

### 3.4 witness的rc3资格语义(第72批→第76批修正)

72批将T2A_14的rc3解释为"对诊断场景的预期资格拒绝"。74审查纠正:rc3源于witness同代际tick回零(结构缺陷),R1修复后此解释不再成立。
