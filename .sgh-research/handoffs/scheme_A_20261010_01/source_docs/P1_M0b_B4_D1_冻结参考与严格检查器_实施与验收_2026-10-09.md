## 3. 新检查器的实际能力与限制

bag reader实际SequentialReader扫描全部storage，零计数topic明确对0，metadata重复字段拒绝，所有未知topic也计数并进入ordinal-framed serialized SHA。逐条Applied、typed job/candidate/Decision/capability、raw input、双路pre-odom/history和change-return、owner、readiness及Witness有对应strict门。source/typed canonical不能变mode或补写null；synthetic正fixture无法提升实际资格。reader有事前消息/字节预算、symlink拒绝和create-only输出。
